# Word Master Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-24

## Summary

Word Master for iOS can be used as a guest app by default, with optional email login for users who want to continue their progress across sessions.

- No account creation is required to start learning.
- No advertising SDK is enabled in the App Store release.
- Learning progress is stored on the device for every user.
- If you choose to sign in, your account email, character profile, and learning progress can also be stored in Supabase.

## Data We Store

Word Master stores the following information locally on your device:

- Quiz history for the current and recent rounds
- Adaptive difficulty and learning progress state
- Audio preference settings
- Character profile preferences

If you choose to sign in, Word Master can also store the following information remotely in Supabase:

- Account email address
- Character profile preferences
- Quiz answer history and adaptive learning progress linked to your account

This local data is used only to support your study experience inside the app.

## Data We Do Not Collect

Word Master does not:

- Use third-party advertising SDKs
- Use tracking for advertising purposes
- Share personal data with data brokers or advertising partners

## Permissions and Tracking

- Word Master does not request App Tracking Transparency permission in the App Store release.
- Word Master does not use advertising identifiers in the App Store release.
