Word Master Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Summary

  • No account is required to start learning.
  • No advertising SDK is included in the App Store release.
  • Learning progress is stored locally on the device.
  • If you sign in, email login, character profile data, and learning progress can also be stored in Supabase.

Local Data

Word Master stores quiz history, adaptive difficulty state, and audio preference settings on your device so the app can continue your study flow across launches.

Remote Data for Signed-In Users

If you choose optional email login, Word Master can store your email address, character profile preferences, and quiz answer history in Supabase so your learning state can continue with your account.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No advertising identifiers
  • No remote analytics or tracking data from the App Store release
  • No data sharing with advertising partners or data brokers
